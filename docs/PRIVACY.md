# SnapRescale privacy policy

SnapRescale does not collect or transmit your images or any personal data.

- The app has no network access and contacts no server.
- It contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising.
- Your images are read from where you open them and written where you save
  them. All processing happens on your Mac.
- If you turn on "Save next to the original without asking", the app asks for
  permission to a folder once and keeps that permission locally as a
  security-scoped bookmark. Settings ▸ Forget All removes these.
- Preferences and presets are stored locally in the app's own container.
- Links in About, Help and Settings open your browser; those sites have their
  own policies.

The app is open source under the MIT licence: https://github.com/phierru/SnapRescale

Questions: https://github.com/phierru/SnapRescale/issues
